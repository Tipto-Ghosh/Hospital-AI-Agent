Hospital Visiting Hours & General Policies – FAQ
=================================================

1. What are the general visiting hours?
   Visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM, seven days a week.  
   Patients in the Intensive Care Unit (ICU) may have restricted visiting times; please check with the nursing station.

2. Are children allowed to visit?
   Children under 12 are permitted in patient rooms only if they are healthy and supervised by an adult at all times.  
   During flu season (October–March), children under 12 may be asked not to visit for their own safety.

3. Can I bring food from outside?
   Yes, but please check with the nursing staff first. Some patients have dietary restrictions, and strong-smelling foods are discouraged.  
   A cafeteria and vending machines are available on the ground floor.

4. What is the hospital’s smoking policy?
   This is a smoke‑free campus. Smoking and the use of e‑cigarettes are prohibited anywhere inside the building or within 25 feet of entrances.

5. How do I reach a patient’s doctor?
   You may leave a message with the nurses’ station, and the doctor will return your call as soon as possible. For non‑urgent matters, you can also use the patient portal.

6. Is there Wi‑Fi available for patients and visitors?
   Yes, free guest Wi‑Fi is available. Connect to the “GuestNet” network and accept the terms of use in your browser.

7. What should I do in case of a fire alarm or emergency?
   Follow the instructions of hospital staff. Do not use elevators. Evacuation routes are posted on every floor.

8. Are there spiritual care or chapel services?
   A multi‑faith chapel is located on the second floor, open 24/7. Chaplains are available on request through the nursing staff.

9. Can family members stay overnight?
   One adult family member may stay overnight in private rooms. For semi‑private rooms, overnight stays must be approved by the charge nurse.

10. What is the policy on service animals and pets?
    Trained service animals are welcome in all public areas and patient rooms. Therapy pets must be pre‑approved and have documented vaccination records.
